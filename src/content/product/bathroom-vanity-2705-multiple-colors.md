## Overview & Design Philosophy

**Model 2705 Bathroom Vanity** is a wall-mounted cabinet defined by a continuous curved front clad in fine vertical fluting. The soft bullnose silhouette softens the wall behind it, while the reeded texture adds depth without visual noise. Floating installation clears the floor completely, so the room reads larger and daily cleaning stays effortless.

The range spans four widths — 1200, 1300, 1500, and 1800 mm — all at a 500 mm depth and 400 mm cabinet height. Every size carries a white ceramic integrated basin and a rounded LED backlit mirror with a copper-free face. The carcase can be built in PVC, MDF, or plywood depending on budget and humidity, and drawer or door layouts suit different storage habits.

## Design Details & Finishing Touches

The cabinet front is machined as a single continuous curve, so the fluting wraps from one side panel around to the other without a visible seam line. Slim black handles are recessed into the top rail of each door or drawer, keeping the profile clean and the front fully flush.

Under the ceramic top, a shadow gap separates basin and carcase and gives the floating body a lighter visual profile. The paired LED mirrors above the double-basin version are rounded rectangles with a slim touch button at the lower centre and a soft halo of backlight. Scene photography shows the vanity styled with a black recessed wall shelf and a rattan pendant lamp, both of which suit the fluted curve.
