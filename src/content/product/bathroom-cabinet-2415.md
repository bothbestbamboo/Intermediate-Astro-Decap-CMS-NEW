## Overview & Design Philosophy

**Model 2415 Bathroom Vanity** is a compact wall-mounted cabinet designed for modern bathrooms where floor space matters. The floating design keeps the floor clear and makes the room feel larger, while the vertically fluted door front adds texture without visual noise.

The vanity is offered in four widths — 500, 600, 700, and 800 mm — all sharing a slim 360 mm depth and 480 mm cabinet height, so it fits everything from a narrow guest WC to a standard family bathroom. The carcase can be built in PVC, MDF, or plywood depending on budget and humidity requirements, paired with a smooth ceramic integrated basin. A recessed channel under the basin works as a hidden handle, keeping the front completely clean. Inside, the cabinet offers open storage sized for daily toiletries and tall bottles.

- **Four Widths, One Slim Depth:** Choose 500, 600, 700, or 800 mm wide — all at a space-saving 360 mm depth that keeps narrow bathrooms comfortable.
- **Three Carcase Options:** PVC for full waterproof performance in wet rooms, MDF for a smooth lacquer finish at an economical price, or plywood for superior screw-holding strength and long-term durability.
- **Space-Saving Floating Design:** Wall-mounted installation frees the floor, simplifies cleaning, and visually enlarges compact bathrooms.
- **Fluted Door Front:** The fine vertical reeding gives the cabinet a furniture-grade look and hides fingerprints better than flat gloss panels.
- **Hidden Handle Channel:** A recessed grip under the basin keeps the front handle-free and snag-safe in narrow bathrooms.
- **Ceramic Integrated Basin:** One-piece easy-clean top with overflow, resistant to stains and daily wear.
- **Organized Interior Storage:** Wood-veneer lined interior fits tall bottles and daily essentials behind a clean front.
