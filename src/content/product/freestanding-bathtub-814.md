## Manufacturing Process

Our solid surface bathtubs follow a meticulous handcrafting and resin casting process:

1. **Material Blending:** High-purity resin is combined with fine mineral compounds for uniform density.
2. **Precision Hand Casting:** Formed inside precision molds to create smooth, ergonomic interior curves.
3. **Seamless Curing & Molding:** One-piece solid molding removes joint seams and potential stress points.
4. **Multi-Stage Hand Polishing:** Expert hand sanding creates a flawless matte tactile texture.
5. **Quality & Leak Testing:** Every tub undergoes strict overflow and hydrostatic pressure testing before final protective packaging.
