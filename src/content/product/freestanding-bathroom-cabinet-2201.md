## Overview & Design Philosophy

**Model 2201 Bathroom Vanity** is a freestanding cabinet with a fine vertical fluted front and three full-width drawers, each finished with a recessed horizontal grip. At 835 mm tall it stands on the floor with a slim recessed plinth, giving the piece the quiet proportion of a joinery unit rather than a wall-hung box.

A round ceramic vessel basin sits on the solid top, paired with a wall-mounted mixer set that keeps the counter completely clear. The fluted front runs continuously across all three drawers, so the texture reads as one surface rather than three separate boxes. Above, a circular backlit mirror with a touch switch spreads even light over the basin area.

Four widths — 600, 700, 800, and 1000 mm — all share a 470 mm depth and 835 mm height, so a compact powder room and a generous family bathroom can share the same design language.

## Advantages & Highlights

- **Three Full-Width Drawers:** Every drawer spans the entire cabinet front, giving wide, unobstructed access for daily toiletries, towels, and cleaning supplies.
- **Recessed Horizontal Grips:** The cut-out channel at the top of each drawer front keeps the face flush and handle-free, so nothing catches on clothing.
- **Freestanding Build:** The cabinet stands on a slim recessed plinth at 835 mm, working with existing floors and plumbing without any wall preparation.
- **Continuous Fluted Front:** Fine vertical reeding runs uninterrupted across all three drawer fronts, softening the silhouette and hiding fingerprints better than flat panels.
- **Clear Countertop:** A wall-mounted mixer set leaves the solid top completely free, so the round vessel basin becomes the only object on the surface.
- **Four Widths, One Depth:** 600, 700, 800, or 1000 mm wide at a consistent 470 mm depth, all in the same 835 mm height.
- **Circular LED Mirror:** The round backlit copper-free mirror with touch switch lights the basin evenly and resists edge corrosion in humid rooms.
- **Three Carcase Options:** PVC for full waterproof performance in wet rooms, MDF for a smooth lacquer finish at an economical price, or plywood for superior screw-holding strength and long-term durability.

## Design Details & Finishing Touches

The fluted panels are cut as one continuous profile per drawer front, and the reeding aligns across the seams so the cabinet reads as a single textured surface from a distance. Between the three drawer fronts, the gaps are cut tight and shadowed, keeping the horizontal lines crisp and even.

At the base, a recessed plinth lifts the carcass slightly off the floor, which protects the cabinet from standing water and makes the floor beneath easier to clean. The solid top is finished in the same colour as the body, or can be specified in a contrasting finish.

Above the basin, the circular mirror carries a copper-free face and a halo of backlighting, with a small touch switch at the lower centre for one-handed control. The wall-mounted mixer set in brushed chrome includes a spout and a separate lever plate, mounted directly to the tiled wall.
