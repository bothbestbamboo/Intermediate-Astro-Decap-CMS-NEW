## Overview & Design Philosophy

**Model 6009** is a 1800 millimetre freestanding bathtub built around a strong visual signature: a raised, sweeping rim that curves upward at both ends like a sculptural vessel. It is cast in one piece from a premium solid surface compound, so the shell has no seams, no joints, and no weak points where water could collect or leak.

The shape is deliberately generous. At 1800 millimetres long and 800 millimetres wide, it is designed for larger bathrooms where the bath is meant to hold the room, not simply fill a corner. The sloped backrests at each end support reclining from either direction, and the central well is deep enough for a full shoulder soak.

Two finishes are offered. The classic version is matte or gloss white throughout, paired with a matte black floor-mounted faucet. The custom version uses a two-tone treatment: a vivid outer shell in gloss red with a pure white interior, finished with a chrome floor-standing faucet set. The contrast is sharp, modern, and immediately recognisable.

## Design Details & Finishing Touches

The body of Model 6009 is shaped from a single cast resin shell. Because it is molded in one piece, the rim, the walls, and the base share the same material all the way through, with no separate skirt or trim piece. That unity is what gives the bath its clean, monolithic look.

The raised rim is the defining detail. It is not a flat ledge but a continuous curve that climbs from the centre drain area toward both ends, forming natural headrests without adding separate cushions or panels. The rim edge is slim and even, keeping the silhouette light despite the large footprint.

In the white finish, the surface reads as a single quiet volume. Paired with a matte black floor faucet and grey stone tiles, it suits restrained, minimalist bathrooms. In the two-tone finish, the red outer skin is glossy and saturated while the interior remains white, so the colour is visible from across the room without overwhelming the bather once inside the tub.
