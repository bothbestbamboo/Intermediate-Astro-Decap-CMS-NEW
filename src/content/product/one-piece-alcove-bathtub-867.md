## Overview & Design Philosophy

**Model 867 One-Piece Alcove Bathtub** is cast from 100% handmade solid surface in a single seamless molding, delivering a clean sculpted silhouette with no joint line anywhere on the body. The asymmetric shell rises gently toward one end to form an ergonomic reclining backrest, while the slim flat deck along the rim carries a chrome mixer, curved spout and hand shower without any visible fixings.

Available from 1400 mm to 1700 mm with a 730 mm width and 720 mm height, this is a deep-soaking bath with the quiet, monolithic presence of carved stone. Shown here in gloss white against large-format grey wall tile and stone-effect floor tile, it works equally well in a private residence or a boutique hotel suite.

## Advantages & Highlights

The advantages of a one-piece solid surface soaking bathtub are:

1. **100% Handmade Solid Surface:** Formed by true one-piece molding with a seamless joint, so there are no seams, stress lines or leak points anywhere on the shell.
2. **Slotted Deep Overflow:** The slotted overflow cutout lets the water level sit higher for immersive shoulder-deep soaking.
3. **Easy to Clean:** Cleans with clean water and a scouring pad — no specialist chemicals or polishing routine required.
4. **Stone-Like Finish:** Like marble or stone, the solid surface is touch smooth, nicely curved and richly textured, in a wide choice of colors.
5. **Repairable Cast Resin:** Matte or gloss surfaces that scratch or chip at the edges can be repaired; video and online support is provided for any restoration.
