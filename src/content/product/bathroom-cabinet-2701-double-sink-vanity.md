## Overview & Design Philosophy

**Model 2701 Bathroom Vanity** is a 1200 mm wall-mounted double sink cabinet built for shared bathrooms. The floating design keeps the floor clear, while the vertically fluted door front and two brushed gold semicircle handles give the cabinet a refined, furniture-like presence.

The double ceramic integrated basin lets two people use the vanity at the same time, and each side has its own faucet position and storage compartment behind the fluted doors. At 465 mm deep and 420 mm high, the cabinet offers generous storage without projecting far into the room. A matching LED backlit mirror with touch control completes the set.
