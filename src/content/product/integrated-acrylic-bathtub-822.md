## Precision Manufacturing Process

Our bathtubs undergo a rigorous 9-stage industrial production workflow to guarantee long-term structural integrity and flawless aesthetic perfection:

1. **Raw Material Inspection & Selection:** Only premium-grade pure acrylic sheets and high-density mineral compounds are approved for production.
2. **Precision Sheet Thermal Heating:** Acrylic panels are evenly heated in advanced thermal ovens to achieve optimal plasticity.
3. **Advanced Vacuum Forming:** Heated sheets are vacuum-formed over precision custom molds to create ergonomic curves and uniform wall thickness.
4. **Controlled Mold Release & Cooling:** Gradual cooling ensures internal stress relief and absolute dimensional stability.
5. **Multi-Layer Fiberglass Reinforcement:** Expert application of high-strength resin and fiberglass composite provides robust load-bearing strength.
6. **CNC Edge Trimming & Hole Drilling:** High-precision automated CNC machinery trims edges and cuts drainage outlets with millimeter accuracy.
7. **Structural Framing & Skirt Docking:** Heavy-duty support frames and exterior panels are securely aligned and clamped for a rock-solid base.
8. **Meticulous Hand Polishing:** Skilled craftsmen execute multi-stage sanding and buffing to deliver a mirror-like, flawless gloss finish.
9. **Rigorous Pressure & Leak Testing:** Every single unit undergoes hydrostatic pressure testing and comprehensive visual inspection before custom protective packaging.
